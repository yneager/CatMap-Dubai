Privacy policy

CatMap Dubai · Last updated 27 October 2026 · Terms of use

CatMap Dubai is a free community app for Dubai's street cats and lost pets, on Android and as a web app. This page explains what the app collects, who can see it, and how you can download or delete it. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

What we collect

Your accountYour email address and password (handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password), username, display name, and the profile photo and details you choose to add. Points, levels and badges are worked out from what you do in the app.
What you postPosts (lost, found, spotted and street animals, adoption), cat profiles, sightings, updates, feeding-station check-ins, rescue reports, sitter profiles, bookings and reviews, adoption requests, reports about content, and the photos you add to any of them.
MessagesYour chats with other members, including photos you send in them.
LocationThe place you choose for a post, sighting or rescue report, and your area for alerts. If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to centre the map and fill in a place for you; it doesn't track you in the background.
SettingsYour app preferences, such as language, notification choices and location privacy. Most of them stay on your device.
Crash reports (Android)If the app crashes, technical details: what went wrong, the phone model, the Android and app version, and a random installation ID. To work out how often the app crashes, it also notes when the app is started. Never your posts, messages, photos or places. You can turn this off in Settings, under Privacy.

Photos

Every photo is re-saved on your device before upload. That removes its hidden metadata, including the GPS position and camera details some phones add. Lists and maps show a small version, and the full photo opens only when someone taps it.

Who can see what

  • Everyone in the app: your username, display name, profile photo, points and badges, your posts, cat profiles, sightings and their photos, and the map position of public posts.
  • Only you: your email address, your settings, your alert area and your inbox.
  • Only the people in a chat: its messages. A message alert, with a short preview, goes only to the recipient's own inbox. Notifications on a locked phone don't show the sender or the text.
  • Volunteers and moderators: the exact place of your sightings, unless you turn off "share exact location with volunteers", and rescue reports, so they can find and help the animal. Moderators also see content that has been reported, to decide whether it breaks the rules.
  • If you block someone, they can't message you or send alerts to your inbox.

Location privacy

When you choose an approximate position, the app moves it to the centre of an area of about 220 metres (or about 500 metres with high privacy) before saving, so the exact spot is never uploaded. Shared posts, posters and alerts name the area, never the position. Rescue reports keep a precise position (about 10 metres), visible only to you, volunteers and moderators.

AI features

  • On your device: the app labels photos (for example "cat") and finds the animal in a photo, using Google's on-device ML Kit. These photos don't leave your device for this.
  • On the CatMap AI computer: when it is switched on, a computer run by the CatMap team checks new photos (what they show, and whether a street animal is already on the map) and compares lost-pet reports with sightings. It receives small copies of the photos involved, and stores a list of numbers describing each post photo, which only the AI computer and moderators can read. The photos sent for a check are deleted from the database once it is answered.
  • AI results are suggestions for people to check. Possible matches are shown only to the owner of the lost pet and the person who posted the sighting.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Hosting, App Check, Crashlytics) stores the app's data and runs the web app. Data may be stored on Google's servers outside the UAE. App Check uses Google Play Integrity (Android) or reCAPTCHA Enterprise (web) to confirm that requests come from the real app.
  • Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap and MapTiler. Like any website, they receive your IP address and which part of the map you're viewing.
  • GitHub hosts the Android app's updates. When the app checks for an update, GitHub receives your IP address.

Notifications

Alerts are kept in your in-app inbox. While the app is open they arrive at once; on Android, a closed app checks the inbox every few hours. You choose which kinds of alerts you get, and you can set quiet hours, in Settings.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and content until you delete them. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. When you log out, the copies the app saved on your device are removed, except unfinished drafts, which wait for you to sign in again.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings, then Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings, then Delete account. This removes your profile and username, your posts and their updates, sightings and their exact places, check-ins, photos, your chat messages, adoption requests, rescue reports, reports you made, your sitter profile, alert areas, favourites, blocks, alerts and private data. Bookings and reviews you shared with someone else stay in their history, with your name and text removed.
  • Change who sees your stats, how precisely your sightings appear, and whether crash reports are sent, in Settings.
  • To ask about your data or to correct it, contact us (below).

Children

CatMap Dubai isn't meant for children under 13, and they shouldn't create an account. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database rules decide who can read and change each piece of data, and they are tested before every release. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we will say so in the app's update notes.
