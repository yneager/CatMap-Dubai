Community rules

CatMap only works if people can trust what they see. Please:

  • Post real animals, real places and honest information. No fake lost-pet reports, sightings or rescue requests.
  • Be kind. No harassment, threats, hate, or bullying in posts, comments, reviews or messages.
  • Don't post other people's personal details, or photos where people are the subject.
  • No spam, advertising, scams, or selling animals for profit.
  • Don't post anything illegal, violent, sexual, or cruel to animals, and follow UAE law, including the rules on animal welfare and on photographing people and private property.
  • Don't try to break, overload or get around the app's security, or collect other members' data.

Animals and safety

  • CatMap connects people; it doesn't check members, animals or posts, and it isn't a vet, a shelter or an emergency service. For an injured animal or an emergency, contact a vet or the relevant Dubai authority.
  • Members, volunteers and sitters are not CatMap staff. Take sensible care: meet in public places, don't share more personal information than needed, and never pay anyone to "return" a pet before you have seen it.
  • AI suggestions, such as "this may be your pet", can be wrong. Always check with your own eyes.
  • Feeding and caring for street animals is your own choice and responsibility. Follow the rules of the place you're in.
